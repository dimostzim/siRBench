# Licensing scope and external dependencies

The MIT licence in `LICENSE` applies to first-party siRBench software and documentation only. It does not relicense experimental source data, adapted upstream implementations, dependency packages, pretrained weights or outputs subject to separate terms. Source provenance, citations, version pins and per-run hashes are retained in the archive.

- **TabPFN-3.5:** the copied Prior Labs licence and model card are under `licenses/TabPFN-3.5/` in the Zenodo package. The licence contains non-commercial restrictions, including conditions for Outputs (section 2d), and restrictions on competitive model training. Those outputs are not covered by siRBench's MIT grant. Foundation weights, fitted TabPFN contexts and fine-tuned weights are external dependencies; obtain authorized access and follow the recorded hashes and reproduction instructions. Gating is not itself a statement that redistribution is forbidden.
- **OligoFormer:** its upstream proprietary licence restricts redistribution and modification. Upstream source, pretrained assets and trained checkpoints are not bundled. The wrapper imports the separately obtained implementation. The packaged terminal-energy helper independently expresses the same calculation; all 256 terminal-pair combinations match the experimental helper exactly. Original frozen copies of that helper are excluded and their hashes recorded. This packaging change does not alter stored data, predictions or results.
- **Rosetta:** source and binaries are excluded; its non-commercial licence is retained for reference. ENsiRNA setup must obtain an appropriately authorized runtime separately.
- **Other published predictors and pretrained assets:** wrapper/source attribution and upstream commit pins are preserved. Separate upstream terms continue to apply. The six published-method trained checkpoint sets are retained in the canonical workspace, not included in this source/data/results ZIP; their sizes, paths and checksums are inventoried. All 228 runs' prediction evidence remains included.
- **Experimental data:** Huesken, Takayuki, Shabalina, Sciabola and Mixset constituent records retain their original provenance and source rights. Data normalization and annotation do not grant new rights in underlying measurements. No blanket MIT licence is assigned to source datasets.
- **Dependencies and journal template:** installed environments, caches and Docker layers are excluded. Package-specific dependency licences and the OUP template's notices remain applicable.

MIT was explicitly selected by the corresponding author for first-party code on 23 September 2026. The package is an archival snapshot, not a transfer of rights in third-party material.
